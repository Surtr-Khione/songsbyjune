Privacy Policy

Last updated July 11, 2026.

What we collect

Order details you give us: your name and email, your recipient's first name, and the story you share for the song. Payments are processed by Stripe; we never see or store your card number.

Reaction videos

Recipients choose whether to record a reaction. Recording only happens after an on-screen yes, with a visible preview the entire time, and the recipient reviews the video before sending. Videos are stored privately and shared only with the purchaser. We only ever use a reaction video in marketing if the recipient explicitly checked the separate social sharing opt-in, and you can withdraw that permission any time by emailing us.

What we never do

We never sell your data, never publish your story or song without permission, and never contact your recipient outside of delivering the gift.

Analytics and advertising

We use privacy-respecting order analytics and standard advertising measurement (such as the Meta pixel) on our marketing pages. Song and reaction pages are private, unlisted links.

Deletion

Email june@songsbyjune.com and we will delete your order data, song files, and any videos within 7 days.
