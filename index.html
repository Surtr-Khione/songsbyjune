Questions

Fair questions, honest answers.

How is this so fast? Custom song companies take weeks.

June writes your lyrics herself from the story you share, then produces the track with modern studio tools instead of booking session musicians for every order. She listens to and approves every song before it ships. The speed comes from the tools. The heart comes from your story, and that part never gets automated.

Will it feel personal, or like a template with a name dropped in?

There are no templates. Your song is written from your answers, so the lyrics carry your actual details: the nickname, the town, the story from that one summer. If the details you gave us are not in the song, that is a rewrite on us.

What if I have no idea what style to pick?

Pick the artist or vibe they already love and June takes it from there. When in doubt, warm acoustic works for almost everyone over 40, and upbeat pop for almost everyone under it.

How does the reaction video work? Is it sneaky?

Not sneaky at all. When your person opens their song page, we ask them on screen if they would like to share their reaction with you while they listen. They can say no and just enjoy the song. If they say yes, they see themselves being recorded the whole time, review the video afterward, and choose to send it. Most people say yes. It is a lovely thing to give back.

What do I actually get?

A private page with their song that you can text or email on the big day, the MP3 to keep, the lyrics, and their reaction video if they record one. Keepsake orders also get a second version of the song, a printable lyric sheet, and a shareable lyric video.

What if I need it TODAY?

That is The Tonight tier. Order by early evening and June moves you to the front of the queue, about 3 hours door to door. Nobody will ever know you remembered this morning.
